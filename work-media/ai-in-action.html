AI in Action Hackathon

A community bridge between local small businesses and nonprofits, regional student talent, and student-built AI.

Start with a real challenge.

The AI in Action Hackathon brings local small businesses and nonprofits together with regional university students. Partners bring a major challenge they are facing; student teams work to build AI solutions that address it.

As founder and lead organizer, I helped bring five universities and four small businesses together in the first six months. The goal is practical collaboration: understand a partner’s needs, connect the right people, and give students the chance to build something with a real-world purpose.

The work is designed to benefit everyone involved: community partners get focused attention on a pressing challenge, and students gain experience listening, collaborating, and applying emerging technology.
