AI and Small Businesses

Putting AI into action for Pittsburgh small businesses—by bringing community partners together around real needs.

October 30, 2026 · 10:00–10:45 AM · Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania · South Concourse

If the embedded recording does not load, watch it on YouTube ↗.

About the talk

Small businesses often face important operational challenges without a dedicated technology, data, or AI team. This talk shares a community-centered approach to closing that gap: bringing small businesses and nonprofits together with students and technology leaders to explore practical AI solutions.

At the center is RMU’s AI in Action Hackathon, where students work on real challenges with guidance from industry and education partners. The presentation also reflects on what the first two years taught the team about building a strong people base, balancing guidance with creative autonomy, broadening partnerships, and keeping real-world needs in view.

01 · ListenUnderstand a business or nonprofit’s challenge before reaching for a tool.
02 · ConnectBring student talent and experienced technology leaders around the problem.
03 · BuildPresent practical solutions and learn from the process together.
